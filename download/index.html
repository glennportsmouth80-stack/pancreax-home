Which phone are you on?

📱 iPhone 🤖 Android

How to get PancreaX on your iPhone

Has to be Safari - Apple only allows this from their own browser.

  1. Open Safari (not Chrome or anything else)
  2. Type in pancreax.com and hit go
  3. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (square with an arrow pointing up ⬆️)
  4. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  5. Tap "Add" in the top right corner

Done - you'll have a PancreaX icon on your home screen like any other app. Always use that icon, not Safari, so your data saves correctly.

How to get PancreaX on Android

Chrome is already the default browser on most Android phones.

  1. Open Chrome
  2. Type in pancreax.com and hit go
  3. Tap the three dots in the top right corner
  4. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app" (wording varies by phone)
  5. Tap "Install" or "Add" when it pops up

Done - PancreaX will sit on your home screen as its own icon. Always use that icon, not Chrome, so your data saves correctly.
